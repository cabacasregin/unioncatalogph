Why a Union Catalog

Connecting a nation's libraries into one discoverable network

Philippine libraries hold rich, distinct collections — but they've historically been searchable only one at a time. Union Catalog Philippines curates and unifies those holdings into a single, shared catalog built for education.

01

Curated Collections

We work with academic, public, special, and national libraries to bring their catalogs into one consistent, well-described record set.

02

Unified OPAC

One search interface across every participating institution — find a title once, see exactly which libraries hold it and how to access it.

03

Built for Education

Designed with students, faculty, and researchers in mind, supporting thesis research, instruction, and lifelong learning nationwide.

How It Works

From scattered shelves to one shared catalog

1

Onboard

A library joins the network and shares its catalog metadata.

2

Curate

Records are cleaned, matched, and standardized for consistency.

3

Unify

Holdings are merged into the union catalog's single search index.

4

Discover

Learners search once and find every library that holds a resource.
